**German Cert Deutschland GmbH** is an independent conformity assessment and certification company headquartered in **Tuntenhausen (Upper Bavaria, Germany)**. Active since **2022**, we provide organizations across manufacturing, logistics, engineering, and services with objective management system audits.

## Our Conformity Assessment Principles

Every issued certificate relies on documented neutrality and evidence-based verification aligned with **DIN EN ISO/IEC 17021-1**:

1. **Documented Impartiality:** GermanCert maintains strict institutional separation between conformity auditing and management consulting — we never advise on systems we assess.
2. **Sector-Qualified Auditors:** Audits are performed exclusively by qualified lead auditors with verifiable industry competence across relevant NACE scopes.
3. **Confidentiality & Data Protection:** Operational metrics, process documentation, and audit findings are safeguarded under binding confidentiality commitments.
