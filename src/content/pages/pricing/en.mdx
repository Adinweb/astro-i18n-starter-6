## Information Needed for a Binding Proposal

1. **Target Standard(s):** Single ISO standard (9001, 14001, or 45001), Integrated Management System (IMS), or GermanCert Trustmark.
2. **Scope & Headcount:** Full-time equivalents (FTEs) operating within the defined certification boundary.
3. **Site Footprint:** Single location or multi-site operations requiring coordinated sampling.
4. **Target Timeline:** Preferred schedule window for Stage 1 readiness and Stage 2 on-site assessment.
