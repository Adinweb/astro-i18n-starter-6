## ISO Management Systems & Integrated Audits (IMS)

All three standards share ISO's **Harmonized Structure**, allowing organizations that operate unified Quality, Environmental, and Occupational Health & Safety systems to undergo a coordinated **Integrated Management System (IMS)** audit.

- **Three-Year Certification Cycle:** Following successful Stage 1 and Stage 2 initial certification, annual surveillance audits verify continued effectiveness in years one and two before formal recertification.
- **Sector-Qualified Audit Teams:** Lead auditors hold verified professional experience across industrial NACE scopes including precision manufacturing, logistics, engineering, and B2B services.
- **Reduced Operational Overhead:** Auditing shared leadership reviews, internal audit programs, and corrective-action workflows in one coordinated schedule avoids duplicate interviews and unnecessary downtime.

</section>

<section id="trustmark" class="scroll-mt-28">

## The GermanCert Trustmark Program

Before committing to a multi-standard ISO cycle, growing businesses and specialized B2B suppliers often need an immediate, credible verification signal for customers and procurement teams. The **GermanCert Trustmark** evaluates organizations across clear governance criteria:

- **Service & Process Consistency:** Documented delivery standards, escalation paths, and structured customer complaint resolution.
- **Compliance & Data Transparency:** Verified statutory disclosures, privacy practices, and clear contractual communication.
- **Operational Continuity:** Evidence of risk preparedness and accountable supplier management.
- **Traceable Seal Issuance:** Every issued Trustmark links to a documented assessment record and a defined annual review schedule.

</section>
