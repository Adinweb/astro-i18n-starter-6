## Native RTL & Bidirectional Localization

Supporting Persian (`fa`) and Arabic (`ar`) alongside English, German, and French requires more than flipping `dir="rtl"` on the document root. Every layout primitive, spacing utility, border, and scroll-reveal vector in this repository is authored exclusively with **CSS logical properties** (`ms-*`, `me-*`, `ps-*`, `pe-*`, `start-*`, `end-*`, `text-start`).

- **Script-Aware Typography & Cursive Protection:** Dedicated variable font stacks (`Vazirmatn` for Persian, `Noto Sans Arabic` for Arabic) activate automatically via `:lang()` selectors while enforcing zero letter-spacing on cursive glyphs so joined characters never fracture.
- **Locale-Native Calendars & Numerals:** Dates and pagination counters format through `Intl.DateTimeFormat` and `Intl.NumberFormat`, rendering Solar Hijri (`persian`) dates and Eastern/Persian numerals natively without external date libraries.
- **Isolated LTR Technical Islands:** Code blocks, mathematical expressions, and email inputs stay isolated in `dir="ltr"` containers while aligning logically to the reading start edge.

## OKLCH Semantic Design System & Progressive Motion

Every colour role—`primary`, `secondary`, `accent`, `neutral`, `info`, `success`, `warning`, and `error`—is defined once in perceptually uniform **OKLCH** colour space in `src/styles/global.css`. Switching between light and dark themes preserves hue identity while guaranteeing **WCAG AA (4.5:1)** contrast across surfaces and foreground pairs.

- **Composable Frosted Surfaces:** Navigation chrome, drawers, and interactive chips share unified `.glass-surface`, `.glass-control`, `.cta`, and `.chip` primitives so visual elevation never drifts across templates.
- **Zero-CLS Progressive Scroll Reveal:** Entrance animations scope strictly under `.js .reveal` using GPU-composited `opacity` and logical `translate3d` transforms, guaranteeing 100% content visibility when JavaScript is disabled or printed.
- **Accessible Smooth Scrolling:** Lenis smooth scrolling coordinates seamlessly with Astro `<ClientRouter />`, nested scrollable drawers (`data-lenis-prevent`), and operating-system `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` preferences.

</section>
