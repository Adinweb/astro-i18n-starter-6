## Bavarian Headquarters, International Reliability

Since 2022, **German Cert Deutschland GmbH** in Tuntenhausen (Upper Bavaria) has supported manufacturing, logistics, and service organizations with objective conformity assessment of their management systems.

Our governance model adheres strictly to **DIN EN ISO/IEC 17021-1** principles: documented impartiality, sector-qualified lead auditors, and evidence-based audit reporting without advisory conflicts of interest.

- **Strict Independence:** Clear organizational separation between conformity assessment and consulting protects the credibility of every issued certificate.
- **Transparent Audit Scoping:** Audit duration is calculated systematically from effective headcount, site structure, and applicable NACE risk classifications.
- **Verifiable Credentials:** Clear certificate records and Trustmark documentation designed for procurement teams, OEM audits, and business partners.
