# First Blog Post

Welcome to our first article! This demonstrates localized slugs and symmetrical content collections.

In this starter:
- Articles are organized by group folders: `src/content/blog/<group>/<locale>.mdx`.
- Slugs are fully translated per language.
- Alternate language links are resolved automatically for hreflang tags and the language switcher.
