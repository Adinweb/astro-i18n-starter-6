Linen & Co. sells hand-woven textiles in Tehran and ships to Europe. Their old site was a single long page that loaded 4 MB of images before showing a price.

We rebuilt it as a static storefront with a real product structure, localized prices, and an editing workflow the owner can run herself.

## What changed

- **Editable content, not a CMS course.** Products are Markdown files next to their photos; adding one is a folder and two files.
- **Prices in both currencies.** Formatters live in one module, so the euro and rial views can never drift apart.
- **Images that fit the page.** Every photograph is optimized at build time, which took the first paint from 6.1 s to 0.9 s on a mobile connection.
