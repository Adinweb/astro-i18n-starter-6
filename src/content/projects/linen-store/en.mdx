## Design Direction

Linen Co. wanted a calm editorial storefront highlighting craft stories, seasonal lookbooks, and transparent material sourcing in both English and Persian.

## Implementation Highlights

- **Cohesive Brand System:** Custom OKLCH color tokens and paired Latin/Persian typography configured cleanly in `app.config.json`.
- **Smooth Motion Choreography:** Lenis inertia scrolling combined with ChronoTrig stagger reveals using logical RTL/LTR offsets.
- **Automated Branded Social Cards:** Deterministic Open Graph PNG generation matching page headlines and brand colors on every share link.
