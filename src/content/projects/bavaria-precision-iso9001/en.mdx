## Context & Certification Scope

Bavaria Precision GmbH operates a multi-shift CNC machining facility in Upper Bavaria employing 85 specialists supplying machinery OEMs across Germany, Austria, and Switzerland. Participating in tiered industrial procurement required formal certification under **ISO 9001:2015**.

## Assessment Scope & Audit Execution

- **Stage 1 Readiness Review:** Inspection of process landscape, calibration register governance, internal audit cycle, and management review records.
- **Stage 2 Shop-Floor Sampling:** End-to-end verification from quotation review and first-article inspection to quarantine control and structured 8D customer complaint resolution.

## Outcome

Following verified closure of two minor documentation observations regarding specialty fixture calibration intervals, Bavaria Precision GmbH received its three-year ISO 9001 certificate with scheduled annual surveillance.
