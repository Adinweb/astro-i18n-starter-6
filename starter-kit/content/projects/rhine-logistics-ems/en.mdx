## Context & Audit Objective

RheinLogistik Süd AG operates three regional distribution hubs across southern Germany. Management sought to harmonize existing environmental and workplace safety controls under **ISO 14001:2015** and **ISO 45001:2018**, complemented by the **GermanCert Trustmark** for B2B partner assurance.

## Combined Audit Execution

- **Environmental Controls:** Fleet energy monitoring, packaging waste segregation, and compliant hazardous-goods containment across warehousing bays.
- **Warehouse Safety Verification:** Traffic separation risk assessments for forklifts, loading-dock ergonomics, contractor safety inductions, and emergency drill records.

## Outcome

Using coordinated **IAF MD 1** multi-site sampling and **IAF MD 5** IMS audit scheduling, the assessment concluded within a single continuous audit week with zero operational downtime.
